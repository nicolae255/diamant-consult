Understanding Biddit:
online public auctions.

How the platform works, key steps, and strategies for buyers and sellers in a notarial public auction in Belgium.

Key points

  • Biddit is the official platform of Belgian notaries for online property auctions; bidders identify themselves with itsme or eID.
  • A bid on Biddit is binding, and not every sale includes a mortgage condition: check your borrowing capacity before bidding.
  • Bidding lasts 8 calendar days; a bid in the last 5 minutes extends the sale by 5 minutes. The buyer then has 6 weeks to pay the price.
  • Public auctions mainly suit inheritances, divorces and forced sales; Diamant Consult organises the viewings on behalf of notaries.

What is Biddit?

Biddit is the official online platform of the Royal Federation of Belgian Notaries for property public auctions. It allows potential buyers to bid online on properties put up for sale by notaries, without having to attend in person. Launched a few years ago, it has become the standard for public auctions in Belgium.

For notaries, Biddit simplifies logistics and widens the pool of potential buyers. For sellers, it guarantees transparency and open competition. For buyers, it provides direct access to auctions from anywhere.

How does a Biddit sale work?

The process follows several well-defined steps:

  1. Listing publication: the notary publishes the property on Biddit with full information (floor area, EPC, title deed, sale conditions). The listing is visible several weeks before the bidding date.
  2. Viewings: open house sessions are organised over 3 weekends. This is where Diamant Consult steps in, we welcome candidates, explain the procedure, answer questions and produce a detailed report after each session.
  3. Biddit registration: to bid, candidates must create a verified account and be identified via itsme or eID.
  4. Bidding period: bids are placed online over 8 calendar days. A bid placed in the last 5 minutes extends the sale by 5 minutes, and so on (notaire.be).
  5. Award: when bidding closes, the award report is signed at the notary's office.
  6. Payment: the buyer has 6 weeks after signing the award report to pay the price (notaire.be).

What buyers need to know

A bid on Biddit is binding: you must be sure you can pay the amount you offer. Not every sale includes a mortgage condition, so check your borrowing capacity with your bank before bidding (notaire.be).

Also budget for purchase costs, mainly registration duties: in Brussels, 12.5% with an allowance on the first €200,000 for a main residence costing no more than €600,000 (notaire.be); in Wallonia, 3% for a sole own home since 1 January 2025 (wallonie.be). The Biddit website has a cost calculator for each property.

Practical tip: visit the property, ask all your questions during open house sessions, and set a firm bid ceiling. The adrenaline of live bidding can push you beyond your budget.

What sellers (and notaries) need to know

Public auctions via Biddit are often chosen in the context of inheritance, divorce or forced sales. They guarantee process transparency and protect all parties against any accusation of favouritism. The final price can surprise on the upside when multiple buyers compete, that's the advantage of an open auction.

On the other hand, it is less suited to sellers who have time and prefer to control the process. A well-managed private sale generally gives the seller more control over the terms (timeline, property condition, signing date).

Biddit public auction costs: who pays what?

This is the question sellers and buyers ask every time. Here is the breakdown:

For the buyer

  • Registration duties: in Wallonia, 3% for a sole own home since 1 January 2025 (wallonie.be). In Brussels, 12.5% with an allowance on the first €200,000 if the price does not exceed €600,000, subject to conditions (notaire.be).
  • Sale costs: the buyer pays the sale costs, mainly registration duties and other taxes, which the notary passes on to the tax authorities (notaire.be). The exact amount is set out in the conditions of sale published on Biddit.

For the seller

  • Costs of the sale process: the seller pays the notary the costs of the public sale (advertising, viewings, organisation). The amount varies case by case (notaire.be).
  • Diamant Consult viewing pack: €600 excl. VAT per file, ordered directly by the notary and invoiced separately.

Frequently asked questions

What is Biddit?

Biddit is the Belgian online platform for public property auctions, run by Belgian notaries (Fednot). Any registered buyer can take part in the auctions from a computer or smartphone, without travelling.

How much does a Biddit public auction cost the seller?

The seller pays the notary the costs of the public sale; the amount depends on the advertising and viewings organised. The buyer pays the sale costs, mainly registration duties. For notaries, Diamant Consult offers a viewing pack at €600 excl. VAT per file.

Is a public auction price always lower than a private sale?

Not necessarily. A well-prepared public auction with organised viewings can generate several simultaneous bids and push the price up. The result depends on the quality of the preparation and how attractive the property is.
