Selling in Ixelles, Uccle
or Etterbeek.

Buyer profiles, price per m², and what makes each municipality different — how Diamant Consult operates in Brussels' most active markets.

Key points

  • Ixelles, Uccle and Etterbeek are three distinct markets: neighbourhoods, property types and buyer profiles all differ.
  • In Ixelles, demand outstrips supply: well-located properties often receive several offers at once, which must be managed well to get the best price.
  • In Uccle, families take more time (repeat viewings, quotes, architects): the sale takes longer, but prices are solid.
  • Etterbeek attracts first-time buyers and investors thanks to its central location; presentation quality makes the difference there.

Three municipalities, three distinct markets

Ixelles, Uccle and Etterbeek are often grouped together as Brussels' "premium" communes. But their property markets are actually quite different depending on the neighbourhood, property type and buyer profile. Here's what you need to know before listing.

Ixelles: Brussels' tightest market

Ixelles is one of the most sought-after markets in Brussels. Well-located properties — around the ponds, Rue du Bailli, Avenue de la Toison d'Or, Place Flagey — can attract several offers at the same time.

The buyer profile is diverse: expats from the European Quarter, young DINK couples (double income, no kids), buy-to-let investors.

In Ixelles, the challenge isn't finding buyers — it's correctly managing several simultaneous offers and choosing the best one. That's precisely what we do: structure the viewing window to create healthy competition and maximise the final price.

Uccle: the market for discerning families

Uccle is the Brussels municipality for families with children: large houses, gardens, proximity to European and international schools.

What differs in Uccle compared to Ixelles: buyers take more time. They visit multiple times, request renovation quotes, consult their architects. The sales process is longer but prices are solid. Patience matters — don't undersell under pressure.

The fastest-selling properties in Uccle are turnkey houses with a decent EPC. Properties needing renovation also find buyers, provided the price honestly accounts for the cost of works.

Etterbeek: the value-for-money option that attracts buyers

Etterbeek enjoys an exceptional central position — steps from the Cinquantenaire, the European Quarter and Brussels-Luxembourg station — at prices still below Ixelles. This draws an informed first-time buyer clientele and investors seeking yield.

Well-located two-bedroom apartments are the most sought-after, and multi-unit investment buildings attract investors.

In Etterbeek, presentation is decisive: buyers compare a lot and are sensitive to photo quality and overall condition. A well-presented property stands out quickly from the rest of the market.

Frequently asked questions

How much is an apartment in Ixelles worth in 2026?

Value depends on the street, floor, condition, EPC and recent comparable sales. For context, the notaries' barometer puts the average apartment price in Brussels at €301,666 over the first nine months of 2026. Diamant Consult provides a free valuation based on comparable sales.

What sells best in Etterbeek?

Well-located two-bedroom apartments, especially near the Cinquantenaire and Brussels-Luxembourg station, are the most sought-after. Multi-unit investment buildings also attract investors.

What is special about the Uccle property market?

Uccle is the market for families with children. Buyers take more time: they visit several times, request renovation quotes and consult their architect. The sale takes longer but prices are solid. Don't undersell under pressure.
