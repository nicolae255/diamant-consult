The Evere property market in 2026

In the first half of 2026, the median house price in Evere was about 20% below the regional median, and the median apartment price about 9% below the regional median (Statbel).

It is above all an apartment market: 9 sales out of 10. Over one year, median prices are up for houses (+13.7%) and stable for apartments (−0.4%). With 33 house sales over the semester, this median can vary sharply from one semester to the next.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to sell my house in Evere?

2% of the sale price excl. VAT, all-in, only if your property sells. The first meeting is free and without obligation.

Do I need a notary?

You do not need to find one: we put you in touch with ours, notary Stephan Borremans, who follows the file from the sale agreement to the deed to keep things moving. In Belgium a sale is always signed at a notary's office, and you remain free to choose your own.

What is my property in Evere worth?

As a guide, in the first half of 2026 the median price was €455,000 for a house and €249,000 for an apartment (Statbel). We set the price of your property with you at the first meeting, based on comparable sales in the neighbourhood, its condition and its EPC.

What if my property does not sell?

You pay nothing: the 2% excl. VAT commission is only due if your property sells.

How long does it take to sell?

The time to a sale agreement depends mainly on the price, the file and how the property is presented. Between the sale agreement and the deed, allow at most 4 months (notaire.be).

Which documents do I need to sell in Brussels?

EPC certificate, soil certificate, planning information and title deed; for an apartment, also the minutes of the last 3 general meetings, the service charge statements and the co-ownership rules. We help you gather them.

Can I sell a rented property?

Yes. The current lease passes to the buyer, and viewings are arranged in agreement with the tenant.

Can you assist me in English or Dutch?

We work in French and English. For Dutch, our partner notary's office and our back office can assist you throughout the sale.
